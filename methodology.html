Methodology and limits

How detection works

Detectors look at statistical patterns. Two common signals are how predictable each word choice is and how much sentence length and structure vary. AI text tends to be more uniform than human writing, though newer models and light editing narrow that gap.

What the labels mean

LabelMeaning
Likely humanFew AI-like patterns found. This does not prove a person wrote it.
MixedSome passages look AI-like and others do not. Common with edited or partly AI-written text.
Likely AIMany AI-like patterns found. This does not prove AI wrote it.

Where detectors go wrong

Formal writing, short texts, non-native English, and heavily edited AI text are all hard cases. False positives and false negatives both happen.

Using results responsibly

Treat a score as one signal. Look at drafts, version history, and the writer’s other work, and talk to the person before drawing conclusions.

Accuracy

We do not publish a headline accuracy figure. When we have our own tested numbers, with the data and method behind them, they will appear on this page.
