Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026. Template text: review it with a lawyer and confirm it matches your detection provider before launch.

What we collect

The text you paste into the checker, so we can score it. Basic technical data such as IP address is processed to apply rate limits and keep the service available.

How your text is used

Your text is sent to a third-party detection provider to produce a score. We do not write submitted text to a database. A short-lived cache keeps a hash of each text with its result, not the text itself.

Third parties

The detection provider processes your text under its own terms and privacy policy. We use analytics and security tools that may set cookies; any such tools will be listed here.

Your choices

Do not submit confidential or personal information you are not allowed to share. To ask a question about your data, contact us.
