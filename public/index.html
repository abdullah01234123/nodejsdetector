How it works

  1. Paste text or upload a .txt file.
  2. Select Check for AI. The passage and sentences are scored by the selected provider.
  3. Read the label and highlights carefully; a detector cannot establish authorship.

Read the full methodology and limits.

What the results mean

Likely human means few AI-like patterns were found. Mixed means some passages look AI-like. Likely AI means many do. None is proof of who wrote the text.

Frequently asked questions

Can it detect ChatGPT text?

It looks for patterns a detection provider associates with AI-generated writing. It cannot identify a model with certainty.

How accurate is it?

Accuracy depends on text type, length, and editing. We do not publish an accuracy number without our own supporting tests.

Can human writing be flagged as AI?

Yes. False positives happen, especially with formal writing or non-native English. Do not treat results as proof.

Can it find AI text that was edited?

Sometimes. Editing and paraphrasing can make AI text harder to detect.

Which languages work?

English is the focus. Other languages depend on the configured provider and may be less reliable.

Is my text stored?

The app does not persist submitted text to a database. In live mode the text is sent to the configured provider; see the privacy policy and that provider's terms.

Is it free?

This software has a character limit and rate limit. A third-party provider may charge for API use.

Can I upload Word or PDF files?

Not currently. Paste text or upload a .txt file.
