Methodology and limits

Chat Detector presents estimates from the configured detection provider. In demo mode, scores are random-looking placeholders and are not detection results.

How detection works

Automated detectors look for statistical patterns associated with machine-generated text. This service relies on its configured provider; we do not independently determine authorship.

What the labels mean

LabelMeaning
Likely humanFewer AI-like patterns were found. This does not prove a person wrote it.
MixedSome passages look AI-like and others do not.
Likely AIMany AI-like patterns were found. This does not prove AI wrote it.

Where detectors go wrong

Formal writing, short texts, non-native English, and heavily edited AI text can be difficult cases. False positives and false negatives both happen.

Use results responsibly

Treat scores as one limited signal. Do not use a result alone to make academic, hiring, disciplinary, or legal decisions.

Accuracy

We do not publish an accuracy figure without independently reproducible testing to support it.
