About Chat Detector

Chat Detector is a simple interface for checking whether text reads as AI-generated, including ChatGPT-style writing.

Our approach

We show a clear label, sentence-level highlights when provided, and the limits of detection. Results are estimates and should not be treated as proof of authorship.

Independence

Chat Detector is an independent project and is not affiliated with OpenAI.
