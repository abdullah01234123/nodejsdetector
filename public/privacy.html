Information processed

The text entered is processed to return a score. Technical information, including an IP address, may be processed by the server and hosting provider for rate limiting, security, and service delivery.

How submitted text is used

The app does not write submitted text to a database. In live detection mode, text is sent to the configured third-party detection provider. The app keeps short-lived results in memory keyed by a cryptographic hash, not by storing the submitted text itself.

Third parties

Your hosting provider and, when enabled, the detection provider process data under their own terms and privacy policies. Check their policies before submitting sensitive information.

Your choices

Do not submit confidential or personal information you are not allowed to share. For questions, contact us.
