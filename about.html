About Chat Detector

Chat Detector is a focused tool for checking whether text reads as AI-generated, including ChatGPT-style writing. It does one job, without sign-ups or clutter.

Our approach

We show a clear label, sentence-level highlights, and the limits of the method, instead of a single number with a promise of certainty.

Independence

Chat Detector is an independent project and is not affiliated with OpenAI.
