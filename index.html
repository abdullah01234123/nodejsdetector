How it works

  1. Paste your text, or upload a .txt file.
  2. Select Check for AI. The passage and each sentence are scored.
  3. Read the label and the highlighted sentences, then decide what to do with them.

What the results mean

Likely human means few AI-like patterns were found. Mixed means some passages look AI-like. Likely AI means many do. None of these is proof, and detectors can be wrong, so use a result as one signal.

Frequently asked questions

Can it detect ChatGPT text?

It looks for patterns common in AI-generated writing, including ChatGPT. It does not identify a specific model with certainty.

How accurate is it?

Accuracy depends on text type, length, and editing. We do not publish an accuracy number until we can back it with our own testing.

Can human writing be flagged as AI?

Yes. False positives happen, especially with formal writing or non-native English, so treat results as one signal among several.

Can it find AI text that was edited?

Sometimes. Heavy editing or paraphrasing makes AI text harder to detect, and a result of Mixed is common.

Which languages work?

English is the focus. Other languages depend on the detection engine and may be less reliable.

Is my text stored?

We do not save it to a database. It is sent to our detection provider to be scored. See the privacy policy.

Is it free?

Yes, within a character limit and fair-use rate limits.

Can I upload Word or PDF files?

Not yet. For now, paste text or upload a .txt file.
