Students and teachers review essays and assignments as part of a conversation about authorship.

How it works

  1. Paste your text or upload a .txt file.
  2. Select Check for AI to request a score.
  3. Read the result carefully; it is not definitive evidence.

Read the methodology and limitations.

What results mean

“Likely human,” “Mixed,” and “Likely AI” are estimates, not proof. False positives and false negatives are possible.

Frequently asked questions

Can it detect ChatGPT text?

It can look for patterns associated with AI-generated writing, but cannot identify a model with certainty.

How accurate is it?

Accuracy depends on text type, length, and editing. Do not treat a score as conclusive.

Can human writing be flagged as AI?

Yes. False positives can happen, especially with formal writing or non-native English.

Is my text stored?

This GitHub Pages version has no working detector backend. When connected to a provider, submitted text may be sent to that provider for scoring.
